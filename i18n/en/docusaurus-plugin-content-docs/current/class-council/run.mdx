The class council sets, student by student, what is printed in the report cards' **Disciplinary observations** and **Principal's observation** for the period. For the annual period, it helps place students in their next classes.

<Procedure>
<Step title="Open the screen">

**REPORTS → Class councils**.

</Step>
<Step title="Choose the period and the class">

Choose the period in the title, then the class: the list of students is shown.

<DocScreenshot src="web/class-council/list" alt="Class council: students of the class, disciplinary decision and overall decision" />

</Step>
<Step title="Enter the decisions">

For each student concerned:

- **Disciplinary decision**: a type (for instance *Félicitations conduite*, *Avertissement conduite*) and the mention to print;
- **Overall decision / Principal**: a type (*Tableau d'honneur*, *Encouragement*, *Avertissement travail*, *Admis en classe supérieure*…) and the mention to print.

The decision types are listed in French. Leave students with no particular decision empty.

<DocScreenshot src="web/class-council/filled" alt="Decisions entered for three fictional students: honour roll, work warning, conduct praise" />

</Step>
<Step title="Save">

**Record the decisions**. A message confirms the save.

</Step>
</Procedure>

<ExpectedResult>

On the report cards generated afterwards, the overall mention replaces the automatic appraisal in **Principal's observation**, and the disciplinary mention is printed in **Disciplinary observations**.

<DocScreenshot src="web/class-council/report-card-effect" alt="Report card of a fictional student: “Honour roll, congratulations” in the Principal's observation" />

</ExpectedResult>

:::tip
Generate the [report cards](/documents/report-cards) **after** the class council. Overall decisions are printed on term report cards since version 2026.11.18.
:::
