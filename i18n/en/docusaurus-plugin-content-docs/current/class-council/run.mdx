The class council shapes the disciplinary and head-of-school remarks shown on the period's report cards. For the annual period, it helps direct students to their next classes.

<Procedure>
<Step title="Open the screen">

**REPORTS → Conseils de classe** (class councils).

<DocScreenshot src="web/class-council/screen" alt="Class council screen: period and class choice" />

</Step>
<Step title="Choose period and class">

Choose the period in the title, then the class: the student list appears in the centre.

</Step>
<Step title="Enter decisions">

For each student, enter the disciplinary and overall decisions taken by the council, then save.

</Step>
</Procedure>

<ExpectedResult>

Decisions appear on the report cards generated afterwards for the period.

</ExpectedResult>

:::tip
Generate report cards **after** the class council so they carry its decisions.
:::
