## Check the address

You sign in at **your school's address**, not on the official website or the documentation. If unsure, [find your school](/getting-started/find-school): its open button leads to the right sign-in page.

## Check username and password

- The username is the **username** given by the administrator, not your e-mail address.
- Passwords are case-sensitive. Check Caps Lock.

## Forgotten password

Ask your **school's administrator** for a temporary password (see [roles and access](/users/roles)). You will change it when you sign in. The BRIDGE team never asks for your password.

## The administrator cannot sign in

[Contact support](/support) from the e-mail address known to the BRIDGE team, giving the school's address. Access is restored after your identity is checked.
