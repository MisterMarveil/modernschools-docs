“Find my school” only shows schools **published** by the BRIDGE team.

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| No result | the school is not published yet, or the name differs | try the short name, town or school code; otherwise contact your administrator |
| **Being prepared** | the new year's space is being put into service | wait: the address will appear once it opens |
| **Suspended** | access is temporarily suspended | contact the school's management |
| **Archive** | only a past year is available | see [archives](/archives/overview) |
| Service temporarily unavailable | search does not respond | try again in a few minutes; if you know your school's address, use it directly |

The administrator of an unpublished school can report it to [support](/support).
