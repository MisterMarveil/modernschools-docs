A commercial code links your registration to the offer or partner who gave it to you.

<Procedure>
<Step title="Request a code (if you have none)">

Open **Request a commercial code** on the official website. Enter your name, school, town, phone or WhatsApp, then your need. **This form creates no school instance**: it records a sales request.

<DocScreenshot src="portal/onboarding/commercial-code" alt="Request a commercial code form" />

</Step>
<Step title="Enter the code in the file">

In the [registration file](/onboarding/new-school), **Offer and terms** step, fill in the **Commercial code** field.

<DocScreenshot src="portal/onboarding/step-5-offer" alt="Offer and terms step: intended offer and commercial code" />

</Step>
</Procedure>

:::info
Terms are agreed with the BRIDGE team: nothing is considered paid on the basis of what the file states.
:::
