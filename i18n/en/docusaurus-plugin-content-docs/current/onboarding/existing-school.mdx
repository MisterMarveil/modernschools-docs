Your school already uses ModernSchools? It **keeps its identity**: do not register again.

<Procedure>
<Step title="Open “My school”">

On `www.modernschools.online`, open **My school**.

<DocScreenshot src="portal/onboarding/my-school" alt="My school page: make a request for my school, access my school" />

</Step>
<Step title="Make a request">

Click **Open a request** for a new-year preparation, a change of name, letterheads or logo, or assistance. The BRIDGE team links the request to your existing school.

</Step>
<Step title="Or go to your school">

The **Access my school** part finds your school and opens its current-year address: see [find my school](/getting-started/find-school).

</Step>
</Procedure>

<ExpectedResult>

The BRIDGE team handles the request; your school's previous years remain available as [archives](/archives/overview).

</ExpectedResult>
