The institutional identity is what appears on report cards, certificates and official documents. It is entered in the **Institutional identity** step of the [registration file](/onboarding/new-school).

<DocScreenshot src="portal/onboarding/step-3-identity" alt="Institutional identity step: official letterhead in French and English" />

## What is requested

- **Official letterhead in French and English**: at most 5 lines, line breaks kept, exactly as it must be printed.
- The school's **motto**, in French and English if it has one.
- **Official logo**: a sharp image (PNG or JPEG). The BRIDGE team derives the document logo, the report-card watermark and the sign-in page image from it.

## Signature and stamp

The signature and stamp are not uploaded on the public website. The BRIDGE team collects them from the authorised signatory; they are kept off the web and embedded in documents when they are produced.

## Changing the identity later

New head of school, short name, letterhead or logo: make a request from [My school](/onboarding/existing-school). Each change creates a new version of the identity; report cards already produced and archives do not change.
