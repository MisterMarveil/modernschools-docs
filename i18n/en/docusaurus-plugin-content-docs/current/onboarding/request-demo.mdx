<Procedure>
<Step title="Open the form">

On the official website, click **Request a demonstration**.

<DocScreenshot src="portal/demo/home" alt="Website home page: Request a demonstration button" />

</Step>
<Step title="Your details and the school">

Full name, role, phone or WhatsApp (required), e-mail; then the school and the city. The approximate number of students and the type of school help prepare a suitable demo.

<DocScreenshot src="portal/demo/contact" alt="Form: contact details and school" />

</Step>
<Step title="Your needs, then send">

Describe what you want to see (grades, report cards, discipline…), choose the language, agree to be contacted, then **Send request**.

<DocScreenshot src="portal/demo/need" alt="Form: needs, preferred language, consent, Send request button" />

</Step>
</Procedure>

<ExpectedResult>

**Request sent**, with an identifier such as `DEMO-2026-000123`: note it, it lets you find your request.

<DocScreenshot src="portal/demo/sent" alt="Request sent, with the request identifier" />

</ExpectedResult>

## Next

The ModernSchools team receives the request and contacts you by phone or WhatsApp to set up the demo. You can also start the conversation right away with the **WhatsApp** button.

:::info
A demo request creates neither a school nor an account. To use ModernSchools, the school then [submits its registration file](/onboarding/new-school).
:::
