Registration takes place on the official website, at your own pace: the file is saved as you go. **It does not activate the school immediately**: every file is checked by the BRIDGE team before the school is put into service.

<Prerequisite>

- A work e-mail address you can access.
- The school's official name, short name and location; the official signatory's contact details.
- The official letterheads (French and English) and the official logo.
- No student photo or personal student data is requested at this stage.

</Prerequisite>

<Procedure>
<Step title="Start the file">

On `www.modernschools.online`, open **Register my school**. Choose your situation (**New school**), enter your e-mail, tick the processing consent, then click **Get my code**.

<DocScreenshot src="portal/onboarding/start" alt="Register my school page: situation, e-mail address, school name" />

</Step>
<Step title="Verify your e-mail address">

Enter the **6-digit code** received by e-mail (valid for 15 minutes), then **Verify and continue**. Note the **file reference** (MS-YYYY-NNNNNN): together with your e-mail, it lets you resume the file on another device.

<DocScreenshot src="portal/onboarding/code" alt="E-mail verification: entering the code received" />

</Step>
<Step title="Fill in the six steps">

**School**, **Contacts**, **Institutional identity**, **Organisation and needs**, **Offer and terms**, **Review and send**. Each field is saved automatically; the progress bar shows how much required information is already provided.

<DocScreenshot src="portal/onboarding/step-1-school" alt="File step 1: the school's administrative identity" />

</Step>
<Step title="Review and send">

The last step lists the missing required information, each with a link. Once everything is filled in, send the file: it can no longer be edited unless the BRIDGE team asks for more information.

<DocScreenshot src="portal/onboarding/step-6-review" alt="Review and send step: list of missing required information" />

</Step>
</Procedure>

<ExpectedResult>

You receive an acknowledgement. The **Follow my file** page shows its progress: sent, under review, more information requested, approved. After approval, the BRIDGE team puts the school into service and gives you its address.

</ExpectedResult>
