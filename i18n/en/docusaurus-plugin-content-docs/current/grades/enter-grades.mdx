<Prerequisite>

A teacher account assigned to the class and subject, and a period whose deadline has not passed.

</Prerequisite>

<Procedure>
<Step title="Open mark entry">

After signing in, click **INSERTION NOTES** (mark entry).

<DocScreenshot src="web/grades/teacher-home" alt="Teacher home: mark entry, my information, sign out" />

</Step>
<Step title="Choose the class, then the subject">

The tiles show your classes; choose the class, then the subject.

<DocScreenshot src="web/grades/teacher-classes" alt="Teacher's class tiles" />

</Step>
<Step title="Prepare the assessment">

The header shows the class, period, subject and its coefficient. Fill in **Compétence attendue** (expected competence), choose the **assessment type** (for example *Evaluation Harmonisée 1*), then click **+** to open the mark sheet.

<DocScreenshot src="web/grades/grade-sheet" alt="Entry mode: marks in 5eme, Term 1, Mathematics coefficient 4; expected competence and assessment type" />

</Step>
<Step title="Enter and save">

Enter each student's mark, then save.

</Step>
</Procedure>

<ExpectedResult>

Marks count towards the period averages and report cards.

</ExpectedResult>

:::info Coefficient
If the coefficient shown seems wrong, tell the administration before entering marks.
:::
