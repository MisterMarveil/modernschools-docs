<Prerequisite>

A teacher account assigned to the class and subject (see [create teacher accounts](/users/create-teacher)), and a period whose mark-entry deadline has not passed.

</Prerequisite>

<Procedure>
<Step title="Choose the period and language">

After [signing in](/getting-started/sign-in), the teacher home shows the **assessment period** (for example *1st Term*) and the **French / English** flags, which change the application language, at the top right. Choose the period before opening mark entry.

<DocScreenshot src="web/grades/teacher-home" alt="Teacher home: assessment period and language at the top right; Marks insertion, My profile, Logout">
  <Hotspot number={1} x={75} y={4.5} label="Period and language" />
  <Hotspot number={2} x={24} y={41} label="Marks insertion" />
</DocScreenshot>

</Step>
<Step title="Choose the class, then the subject">

Click **MARKS INSERTION**: your classes appear. Choose the class, then the subject.

<DocScreenshot src="web/grades/teacher-classes" alt="Teacher's class tiles" />

</Step>
<Step title="Check the coefficient and enter the expected competence">

The header shows the class, period, subject and its **coefficient**. Enter the **expected competence** for the assessment (*Compétence attendue*): it is saved for the class, subject and period, and suggested for the next sheet.

<DocScreenshot src="web/grades/sheet-competence" alt="Sheet header: entry mode, 5eme, 1st Term, Mathematics, COEF 4; expected competence filled in" />

</Step>
<Step title="Choose the assessment type and open the sheet">

Choose the **assessment type** (for example *Harmonized Evaluation 1*), then click **+**.

<DocScreenshot src="web/grades/grade-sheet" alt="Assessment type and + button to open a new sheet" />

</Step>
<Step title="Enter the marks and submit">

Enter each student's mark **out of 20** (to the quarter point). The **appraisal** is computed from the school's grid. The icon next to the mark flags a student as **absent**. Click **Soumettre** (submit).

<DocScreenshot src="web/grades/grade-filled" alt="Mark sheet: one mark out of 20 per student, appraisal and remark computed" />

</Step>
</Procedure>

<ExpectedResult>

A message confirms the marks were saved. The sheet now appears as a column header when you reopen the class and subject.

<DocScreenshot src="web/grades/grade-saved" alt="Confirmation that the marks were saved" />

</ExpectedResult>

## The coefficient is missing or looks wrong

- **Wrong coefficient**: tell the administration before entering marks; it corrects it.
- **No coefficient** for the subject in this class: ModernSchools lets you **suggest a coefficient**. Enter the value, then **Soumettre la suggestion** (submit). It applies at once so you can enter marks, and remains subject to the administration's approval.
