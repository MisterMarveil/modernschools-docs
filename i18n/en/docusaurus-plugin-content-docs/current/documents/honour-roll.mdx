<Procedure>
<Step title="Open the screen">

**REPORTS → Honour rolls**.

</Step>
<Step title="Choose the period and the class">

Choose the period in the title, then the class. ModernSchools lists the deserving students, from the best average down: those whose period average reaches **12/20**.

<DocScreenshot src="web/documents/honour-roll" alt="Honour rolls: class chosen, list of deserving students" />

</Step>
<Step title="Generate">

**Generate honour roll**, then **OK**. For a single student, first pick them in **Choose a student**. Progress is shown student by student.

<DocScreenshot src="web/documents/honour-roll-confirm" alt="Confirmation of the class honour roll generation" />

</Step>
<Step title="Download">

When it finishes, the PDF appears in the explorer on the right, **Honour-Roll** folder.

<DocScreenshot src="web/documents/honour-roll-done" alt="Explorer: the honour roll PDF in the Honour-Roll folder" />

</Step>
</Procedure>

<ExpectedResult>

A landscape A4 PDF, one page per student: school letterhead, student name, class, period, average and mention.

<DocScreenshot src="web/documents/honour-roll-pdf" alt="Honour roll certificate of a fictional student" />

</ExpectedResult>
