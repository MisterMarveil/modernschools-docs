The minutes sum up, for a class and a period, each student's marks by subject and evaluation, their average, rank and observation.

<Procedure>
<Step title="Open the screen">

**REPORTS**, **Performance** column: **Results sheets**.

<DocScreenshot src="web/documents/reports-home" alt="Reports home: Performance column" />

</Step>
<Step title="Choose the period and the class">

Choose the period in the title (for instance **1st Term**), then the class.

<DocScreenshot src="web/documents/results-minutes" alt="Results minutes generation: period, class, View, Generate PDF" />

</Step>
<Step title="View">

**View**, then confirm: the minutes open in a new tab.

<DocScreenshot src="web/documents/results-minutes-view" alt="Minutes on screen: students, marks by subject and evaluation, average, rank" />

</Step>
<Step title="Generate the PDF">

**Generate PDF**, then confirm. The file appears in the explorer on the right, **bordereaux** folder: click it to download. With no class chosen, the PDF covers the whole school.

<DocScreenshot src="web/documents/results-minutes-done" alt="Explorer: the class minutes in the bordereaux folder" />

</Step>
</Procedure>

<ExpectedResult>

A landscape A3 PDF, with the school's letterhead, the ranked list of students and the class summary.

<DocScreenshot src="web/documents/results-minutes-pdf" alt="PDF minutes of a fictional class" />

</ExpectedResult>

:::tip
A corrected mark: **Clean up** the explorer, then generate again. First check that every mark is entered with the [grade entry status](/analytics/overview).
:::
