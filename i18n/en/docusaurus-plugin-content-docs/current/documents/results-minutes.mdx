<Procedure>
<Step title="Open the screen">

From **REPORTS**, **Performances** column, open the results-minutes generation.

<DocScreenshot src="web/documents/results-minutes" alt="Results minutes generation" />

</Step>
<Step title="Choose period and class">

Choose the period in the title, then the class.

</Step>
<Step title="View or generate">

**Visualiser** shows the minutes on screen; **Générer PDF** produces the document to print.

</Step>
</Procedure>
