From the administrator home, click **REPORTS**. The screen groups outputs in three columns.

<DocScreenshot src="web/documents/reports-home" alt="Reports screen: Performances, Secrétariat and Factory columns" />

| Column | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Performances** | report cards, results sheets, honour rolls, class councils, mark-entry status, statistics |
| **Secrétariat** (office) | staff and student lists, timetables, start-of-duty, attendance, transfer, forms |
| **Factory** | school ID cards, report-card covers, badges, exam photos, certificate of enrolment |

Documents are produced for **one period** and **one class**; generated files appear in the **bordereaux** box on the right, where you download them. **Nettoyer** (clean) empties that box.

The document screens are currently in French in both languages.
