<Prerequisite>

Student photos are imported: see [import student photos](/students/photos).

</Prerequisite>

<Procedure>
<Step title="Open the screen">

**REPORTS → Cartes id. scolaires** (school ID cards).

<DocScreenshot src="web/documents/id-cards" alt="School ID cards screen: automatic or manual mode, class, view, generate PDF" />

</Step>
<Step title="Choose the mode">

- **Mode Automatique**: a whole class (chosen under **Choisir la classe**) or, with **Tout générer**, the whole school.
- **Mode Manuelle**: a specific list of students.

The **Établi(e)s le** field sets the issue date printed on the cards.

</Step>
<Step title="View, then generate">

**Visualiser Cartes Scolaires** to check, then **Générer Cartes Scolaires - PDF**.

</Step>
</Procedure>

<ExpectedResult>

A print-ready PDF, with the photo of every student who has one.

</ExpectedResult>
