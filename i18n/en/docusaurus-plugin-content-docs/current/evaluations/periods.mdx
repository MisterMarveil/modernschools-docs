- The **Annuelle** period covers the school year.
- Its **child periods** are the terms (or semesters, depending on the chosen template).
- Each period has a start date, an end date and a **mark-entry deadline**: after that date, entry is closed for teachers.
- Marks are entered by **assessment type** (for example *Evaluation Harmonisée 1*) within a period. Types are managed under **Assessment grids → Assessment type**.

Report cards, results minutes, honour rolls and class councils are produced **for one period**.
