<Procedure>
<Step title="Open the period">

**Settings → Structures → Assessment periods**, then **Edit** on the period (for example *Trimestre 1*).

</Step>
<Step title="Change the dates">

<DocScreenshot src="web/evaluations/period-edit" alt="Editing period Trimestre 1: parent period, dates, deadline, name and short name" />

Fields: **parent period**, **start date**, **end date**, **mark-entry deadline**, **period name** and **short name**.

</Step>
<Step title="Save">

**Update**.

</Step>
</Procedure>

<ExpectedResult>

The new deadline applies immediately to mark entry for that period.

</ExpectedResult>

:::warning
Tell teachers before bringing a deadline forward: entry closes on that date.
:::
