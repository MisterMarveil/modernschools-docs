<Procedure>
<Step title="Find the student">

**Settings → Users → Students**. Use **Filters** to search by surname, first name or class, then click **Edit** on the student's row.

</Step>
<Step title="Edit">

Correct the fields in the **Personal data** and **Additional data** tabs.

<DocScreenshot src="web/students/edit" alt="Editing student SAMBA MEKONG Martin">
  <Hotspot number={1} x={45} y={27} label="Class" />
  <Hotspot number={2} x={20} y={73} label="Update" />
</DocScreenshot>

</Step>
<Step title="Save">

Click **Update** (or **Update and close**).

</Step>
</Procedure>

<ExpectedResult>

The list shows the new details. The matricule does not change.

</ExpectedResult>
