<Prerequisite>

Students are already registered. Open **Settings → Configuration initiale → Ouvrir**, section **4. Photos d'élèves** (student photos), then **Importer des photos**.

</Prerequisite>

<DocScreenshot src="web/photos/index" alt="Student photos page: ZIP upload, preparation guidance and list of batches" />

## Prepare the ZIP

One folder per class, named like the class in ModernSchools; one photo per student, named after their **surname and first names**, or their **matricule**.

```text
photos-2026-2027.zip
├── 6eme/
│   ├── SURNAME_FIRSTNAMES.jpg
│   └── MATRICULE.jpg
└── Tle D/
    └── SURNAME FIRSTNAMES.png
```

- JPEG, PNG or WebP; 15 MB at most per photo; 3,000 photos at most per batch. Export iPhone HEIC photos to JPEG first.
- No facial recognition: a photo is matched to a student only through the file name.

<Procedure>
<Step title="Upload the ZIP">

Under **Déposer un ZIP** (upload a ZIP), choose the file and send it. Analysis runs in the background: the batch page updates by itself. Only one batch can be in progress at a time.

</Step>
<Step title="Check the class table">

Each ZIP folder is linked to a class of the year (**automatique** when the name matches). Correct the class in the list if needed.

<DocScreenshot src="web/photos/proposed" alt="Batch to check: class table, analysis and proposed photos with the recognised student" />

</Step>
<Step title="Decide on photos to review">

**À revoir** tab (to review): similar name, several possible students, no student in the class, image without a student name, duplicate, or student who already has a photo. For each, choose the student or leave *à décider (non appliquée)* (undecided, not applied), then **Enregistrer les décisions** (save decisions).

<DocScreenshot src="web/photos/review" alt="To review tab: reason for each photo and choice of student" />

</Step>
<Step title="Validate the batch">

Under **Validation du lot**, tick the box confirming you checked that each retained photo is that of the student shown, then **Valider le lot** (validate). **Abandonner le lot** cancels it without saving anything.

<DocScreenshot src="web/photos/validate" alt="Batch validation: number of photos saved, confirmation box, Validate button" />

</Step>
</Procedure>

<ExpectedResult>

Retained photos are cropped to portrait (3:4) and appear on students' documents, for example school ID cards. Photos left *undecided* are not saved: you can include them in a later batch.

</ExpectedResult>

:::info Privacy
Photos stay private: only signed-in administrators can see them, and they are embedded in documents when those are produced. Originals are kept. An applied batch can be cancelled: the previous photos come back.
:::
