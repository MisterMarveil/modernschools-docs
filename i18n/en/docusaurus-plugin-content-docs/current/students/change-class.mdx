<Procedure>
<Step title="Open the student's record">

**Settings → Users → Students → Edit** on the student's row.

</Step>
<Step title="Choose the new class">

Under **Données de l'établissement** (school data), choose the new **Class**, then **Update**.

<DocScreenshot src="web/students/edit" alt="Class field in the student's record">
  <Hotspot number={1} x={45} y={27} label="New class" />
</DocScreenshot>

</Step>
</Procedure>

<ExpectedResult>

The student appears in the lists, mark entry and documents of the new class.

</ExpectedResult>

:::warning During a period
Marks already entered stay with the student. Before a move during a period, tell the teachers of both classes and check the report cards concerned.
:::
