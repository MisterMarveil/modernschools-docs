<Procedure>
<Step title="Open the editable list">

On the **reports** page, under **Gestion des profils** (profile management; this page is not translated yet), click the **Elèves** (students) slice of the chart.

<DocScreenshot src="web/students/batch-pie" alt="Reports page: user breakdown chart, Students slice" />

</Step>
<Step title="Choose a class">

At the top, choose the class, or search for a student by surname, first name or registration number.

</Step>
<Step title="Correct">

Every field can be edited in place: registration number, surname, first names, date and place of birth, gender, class. Make the change, then leave the field: it is **saved immediately**, and a notification confirms it at the top right. Changing the class of a student who has marks asks for confirmation: see [move a student to another class](/students/change-class).

<DocScreenshot src="web/students/batch-saved" alt="Bulk edit: first name corrected and save notification" />

</Step>
<Step title="Add a photo">

On the student's row, click the button in the **Image** column and choose the photo: it appears as soon as it is saved.

</Step>
</Procedure>

<ExpectedResult>
The corrections appear in the next lists, report cards and student ID cards. There is no "Save" button: each field you leave is already saved.
</ExpectedResult>

:::tip
For a whole school's photos, use the [ZIP photo import](/students/photos) instead: automatic matching and a check before validation.
:::
