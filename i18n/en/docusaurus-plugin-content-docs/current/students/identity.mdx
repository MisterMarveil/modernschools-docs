Every student receives a **unique matricule** (student number), assigned automatically by ModernSchools when the student is saved (entered or imported). In the demo school it looks like `26N0001`.

## What it is for

- Telling apart two students who look alike.
- Finding a student in lists and documents: report cards, school ID cards.
- Matching a photo to a student: a file named after the matricule is recognised unambiguously during [photo import](/students/photos).

## Good to know

- The matricule does not change when the student changes class or when their details are corrected.
- When [importing a list](/students/import), the *Matricule* column may be left empty: it is then assigned automatically.
- This is the ModernSchools matricule. It does not replace an identifier issued by another administration.
