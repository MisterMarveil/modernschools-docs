<Prerequisite>

The classes and subjects exist (see [initial setup](/structure/initial-setup)). Open **Settings → Initial setup → Open**, section **3. Teachers and assignments**.

</Prerequisite>

<DocScreenshot src="web/setup/teachers-import" alt="Teachers and assignments section: template to download, Check without importing, Import" />

<Procedure>
<Step title="Download the template">

Click **modele-enseignants.xlsx**. It holds your subjects (*Matieres* sheet, drop-down list) and your classes (*Classes* sheet). The column titles are in French.

</Step>
<Step title="One row per teacher and per subject">

**Nom** (last name), **Prénom** (first name), **Sexe**, **Matière** (subject) and the **Classes** where they teach it, separated by commas. A teacher with two subjects takes two rows, with the same name written the same way.

Optional: the **coefficient**, only if the subject is not yet on the class's syllabus; the class they are **head teacher** of; their **phone** and **e-mail** (useful for grade-entry reminders).

<DocScreenshot src="web/setup/teachers-template-filled" alt="Filled template: two fictional teachers, one row per subject, classes, coefficient, head teacher" />

</Step>
<Step title="Check without importing">

**Check without importing** verifies the whole file: unknown class, subject off the syllabus without a coefficient, invalid e-mail, duplicate… Each error is given with its row number.

<DocScreenshot src="web/setup/teachers-check-errors" alt="Check: unknown class row 4, subject off the syllabus without a coefficient row 5" />

Once the file is correct, the check announces the teachers and assignments to create.

<DocScreenshot src="web/setup/teachers-check-ok" alt="File correct: 2 teachers and 5 assignments to create" />

</Step>
<Step title="Import">

Send the same file again with **Import**.

</Step>
</Procedure>

<ExpectedResult>

The teachers are created in the *Enseignants* (teachers) group. Each signs in with the username **LASTNAME Firstname** and the teachers' default password, to be changed at first sign-in (see [roles and accounts](/users/roles)). They find their classes and subjects in grade entry.

<DocScreenshot src="web/setup/teachers-imported" alt="2 teachers created, 5 assignments created, reminder of the username and default password" />

</ExpectedResult>

:::info Provisional timetable
The import puts one session per class and subject in the timetable: then adjust the times (see [build the timetable](/timetable/build)). A teacher or assignment already present is not created again.
:::
