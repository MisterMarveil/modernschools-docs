## By import (recommended)

<DocScreenshot src="web/setup/teachers-import" alt="Teachers and assignments section of the initial setup" />

<Procedure>
<Step title="Download the template">

**Settings → Configuration initiale → Ouvrir**, section **3. Enseignants et affectations** (teachers and assignments): download **modele-enseignants.xlsx**. It contains your subjects and classes.

</Step>
<Step title="Fill it in">

One row per teacher **and per subject**, with the classes where they teach it, separated by commas.

</Step>
<Step title="Check, then import">

**Contrôler sans importer** (check), then **Importer**. The file is checked as a whole: at the slightest error, nothing is imported.

</Step>
</Procedure>

<ExpectedResult>

Each teacher gets an account and finds their classes and subjects in mark entry. The timetable created is provisional (one session per class and subject): adjust the times afterwards.

</ExpectedResult>

## By hand

**Settings → Users → Users → Add**. Fill in **Lastname**, **Firstname**, the **username** (used to sign in) and the **password** twice, then choose the *Enseignants* (teachers) group in the **Additional data** tab.

Hand the username and temporary password to the teacher in person. At first sign-in, they will have to choose their own password.
:::
