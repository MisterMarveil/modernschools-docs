## By import (recommended)

<DocScreenshot src="web/setup/teachers-import" alt="Teachers and assignments section of the initial setup" />

<Procedure>
<Step title="Download the template">

**Settings → Configuration initiale → Ouvrir**, section **3. Enseignants et affectations** (teachers and assignments): download **modele-enseignants.xlsx**. It contains your subjects and classes.

</Step>
<Step title="Fill it in">

One row per teacher **and per subject**, with the classes where they teach it, separated by commas.

</Step>
<Step title="Check, then import">

**Contrôler sans importer** (check), then **Importer**. The file is checked as a whole: at the slightest error, nothing is imported.

</Step>
</Procedure>

<ExpectedResult>

Each teacher gets an account and finds their classes and subjects in mark entry. The timetable created is provisional (one session per class and subject): adjust the times afterwards.

</ExpectedResult>

## By hand

For a teacher, but also an accountant, a studies dean or a discipline supervisor: the chosen group sets the account's role.

<Procedure>
<Step title="Open the form">

**Settings → Users → Users**, then **Add new**.

<DocScreenshot src="web/users/list" alt="User list, Add new button at the top right" />

</Step>
<Step title="Identity and username">

**Personal data** tab: **Lastname**, **Firstname**, **Gender**, and the **username**, the one used to sign in. The form asks for no password.

<DocScreenshot src="web/users/create-identity" alt="Personal data tab: lastname, firstname, gender, username, no password field" />

</Step>
<Step title="Choose the group">

**Additional data** tab, **Users' Groups**: tick the group, for instance *Teachers*, then **Create**. Without a group, creation is refused and the section is marked in red.

<DocScreenshot src="web/users/create-no-group" alt="Creation refused: Users' Groups marked in red" />

<DocScreenshot src="web/users/create-group-checked" alt="Teachers group ticked" />

</Step>
</Procedure>

<ExpectedResult>

The "… has been created" message shows and the account appears in the list.

<DocScreenshot src="web/users/created" alt="Account created" />

</ExpectedResult>

:::warning Password
No password is typed: the person signs in with their username and their group's default password (`profpass` for teachers), then chooses their own. If forgotten: **Actions → Reset password** on their record. See [default passwords](/users/roles#default-passwords).
:::

Hand the username over in person. The default password is never sent by e-mail or messaging.
