Rights are granted by **user group** (**Settings → Users → User groups**).

<DocScreenshot src="web/users/list" alt="User list with their group: Administrateurs Opérationnels, Enseignants" />

| Group | For whom | What it allows |
|---|---|---|
| Administrateurs Opérationnels | the platform administrator within the school | configure the school: structure, users, assessments, documents |
| Administrateurs VIP | school management | view all statistical reports and school data |
| Enseignants (teachers) | teachers | enter marks for their classes and subjects, suggest a missing coefficient, manage their profile |
| Responsable des études (head of studies) | the censor or head of studies | [build timetables](/timetable/build), [manage coefficients, weekly hours and expected competences](/timetable/constraints), approve suggested coefficients |

## A user can no longer sign in

1. **Settings → Users → Users**, then **Edit** on their row.
2. Enter a new temporary password (twice), then **Update**.
3. Hand it to the user in person: they will change it when they sign in.

Never send a password by e-mail or messaging app. See also [sign-in problems](/troubleshooting/sign-in).
