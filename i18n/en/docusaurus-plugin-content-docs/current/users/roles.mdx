Rights are granted by **user group** (**Settings → Users → User groups**).

<DocScreenshot src="web/users/list" alt="User list with their group: Administrateurs Opérationnels, Enseignants" />

| Group | For whom | What it allows |
|---|---|---|
| Administrateurs Opérationnels | the platform administrator within the school | configure the school: structure, users, assessments, documents, reports |
| Administrateurs VIP | school management | view all statistical reports and school data |
| Enseignants (teachers) | teachers | enter marks for their classes and subjects, suggest a missing coefficient, manage their profile |
| Responsable des études (head of studies) | the censor or head of studies | [build timetables](/timetable/build), [manage coefficients, weekly hours and expected competences](/timetable/constraints), edit the students of their classes, approve suggested coefficients |
| Chargés de discipline (discipline officers) | the head of discipline, supervisors | [enter discipline information](/discipline/enter-info), [write to families](/discipline/messaging) |
| Comptables (accounting) | the bursar | manage their profile (the accounting module is not available yet) |

## One profile at a time

Each page belongs to a profile: a discipline officer opens neither reports, timetables nor student editing. An account with several groups (teacher and head of studies, for example) chooses its profile after signing in; it then opens only that profile's pages, and can switch from the home page.

A page reserved for another profile shows a message explaining it, and offers to switch to the right profile when the account has it.

<DocScreenshot src="web/roles/refus-profil" alt="Restricted page: reserved for the administration, the user is signed in as head of studies" />

## Limit an account to class groups

A head of studies or a discipline officer can be limited to part of the school, for example the first cycle. In the user's record, **Additional data** tab, choose the class groups under **Authorized intervention area** (Niveau de compétence); sub-groups are included.

<DocScreenshot src="web/roles/admin-groupes" alt="User record: intervention area and user group">
  <Hotspot number={1} x={71} y={28} label="Intervention area" />
</DocScreenshot>

The account then only sees the classes of these groups: class lists, students, timetables, constraints, discipline. Any action on another class is refused with a message naming the class and the assigned groups. Without an intervention area, the account works on all classes.

<DocScreenshot src="web/roles/censeur-hors-perimetre" alt="Outside your class groups: class Tle D is not part of the first cycle" />

## Default passwords

A new account, or one whose password is reset, gets its group's default password. At sign-in, the person must replace it with their own ([sign in](/getting-started/sign-in)): the application stays closed until they do.

| Group | Default password |
|---|---|
| Administrateurs Opérationnels, Administrateurs VIP | `admin` |
| Responsable des études | `censeurpass` |
| Chargés de discipline | `surpass` |
| Comptables | `comptapass` |
| Enseignants | `profpass` |

An account with several groups gets the password of the first group in this list.

## A user can no longer sign in

1. **Settings → Users → Users**, then **Edit** on their row.
2. **Actions** menu → **Reset password**, then confirm.
3. Give the user their **username**: they sign in with their group's default password, then choose a new one.

<DocScreenshot src="web/roles/admin-reinitialiser" alt="User record: Actions menu, Reset password">
  <Hotspot number={1} x={91} y={22} label="Reset password" />
</DocScreenshot>

The administration never types or sends a personal password. See also [sign-in problems](/troubleshooting/sign-in).
