<Prerequisite>

An account in the **Responsable des études** (head of studies) group. From the home screen, click **CONSTRAINTS SETTINGS**.

</Prerequisite>

## Coefficient and weekly hours

<Procedure>
<Step title="Choose the subject and class">

Choose the **subject**, then the **class**: the current **weekly hours** and **coefficient** appear.

<DocScreenshot src="web/constraints/constraints-selected" alt="Constraints: Mathematics, 5eme, weekly hours 4, coefficient 4" />

</Step>
<Step title="Change">

Change the hours or the coefficient: the value is **saved at once** and a message confirms it. The new coefficient counts in averages and report cards; it is shown at the top of the teacher's mark sheet.

<DocScreenshot src="web/constraints/constraints-quota-saved" alt="Weekly hours set to 5: data saved successfully" />

</Step>
</Procedure>

## Expected competence

For the chosen subject and class, select the **period** (for example *1st Term*), enter the **expected competence** at the end of the period, then **Sauvegarder** (save). Tick the propagation box to apply it to other classes.

<DocScreenshot src="web/constraints/constraints-competence" alt="Expected competence for the 1st term, propagation box and save button" />

The teacher finds this competence pre-filled on the mark sheet and can refine it.

## Summary

**aperçu** (preview) shows the table of weekly hours (H) and coefficients (C) by subject and class; **Générer le PDF** produces the printable document.

<DocScreenshot src="web/constraints/constraints-preview" alt="Constraints preview: weekly hours and coefficients by subject and class" />

## Approve suggested coefficients

When a teacher [suggests a coefficient](/grades/enter-grades#the-coefficient-is-missing-or-looks-wrong), a window announces it on the head of studies' home screen.

<DocScreenshot src="web/timetable/censeur-home-pending" alt="Pending suggestions window: proceed to validation or later" />

The validation button opens the list: check each value, correct it if needed under **Coefficient validé** (a whole number from 1 to 50), then **Valider les coefficients** (approve).

<DocScreenshot src="web/constraints/coef-pending" alt="Pending coefficient suggestions: teacher, class, subject, suggested and approved coefficient" />
