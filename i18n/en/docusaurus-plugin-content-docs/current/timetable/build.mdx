<Prerequisite>

An account in the **Responsable des études** (head of studies) group: see [roles and access](/users/roles). Classes, subjects and teachers already exist (see the [initial setup](/structure/initial-setup)).

</Prerequisite>

<Procedure>
<Step title="Open timetables">

After signing in, the head of studies' home offers **TIMETABLE BUILDING**, **CONSTRAINTS SETTINGS**, **STUDENT MANAGEMENT**, **MY PROFILE** and **LOGOUT**. Click **TIMETABLE BUILDING**.

<DocScreenshot src="web/timetable/censeur-home" alt="Head of studies home: timetable building, constraints settings, student management, my profile, logout" />

</Step>
<Step title="Choose the class, then the teacher">

On the left, the **subjects**; in the centre, the **Class** and **Teachers** choices; on the right, the **weekly grid** (time slots and breaks). Choose the class: the grid shows all its sessions. Then choose the teacher: only their sessions in that class remain.

<DocScreenshot src="web/timetable/timetable-loaded" alt="Weekly grid for class 5eme and teacher NDZANA Pierre" />

</Step>
<Step title="Place the sessions">

- **Add**: drag a subject from the left-hand list onto the slot.
- **Move**: drag a session from one slot to another.
- **Remove**: drag it into the **Zone de suppression** (delete zone) below the grid.

Break slots cannot hold a session.

<DocScreenshot src="web/timetable/timetable-dragged" alt="Mathematics sessions moved to Tuesday and added on Thursday" />

</Step>
<Step title="Save">

Click **Sauvegarder** (save). The teacher's grid for this class replaces the previous one.

<DocScreenshot src="web/timetable/timetable-saved" alt="Confirmation: data saved successfully" />

</Step>
</Procedure>

<ExpectedResult>

A message confirms the save. The teacher finds this class and subject in [mark entry](/grades/enter-grades): only a teacher who appears in a class's timetable can enter marks for it.

</ExpectedResult>

:::info Provisional timetable
The [teacher import](/users/create-teacher) creates one session per class and subject, on Monday: that is what opens mark entry. Rearrange those sessions here afterwards.
:::
