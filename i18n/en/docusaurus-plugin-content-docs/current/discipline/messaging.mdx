:::info Service to be enabled
Sending goes through a delivery service (WhatsApp, e-mail) that ModernSchools connects school by school. Until it is connected, the page says so: you can prepare a message but not send it.
:::

<Procedure>
<Step title="Choose the recipients">

On the home page, **Messagerie**. Choose the **class**: its students are listed with the number of family contacts (phone, e-mail). Tick the students concerned, or **Whole class**.

<DocScreenshot src="web/roles/messagerie" alt="Messages to families: class, students and contacts, Parents summoned template, preview" />

</Step>
<Step title="Write">

Choose **WhatsApp**, **e-mail** or both, then a **template** (absence, late arrival, summons, detention) or a free message. `{eleve}`, `{classe}` and `{etablissement}` are replaced for each student; the **preview** shows the first ticked student's message.

</Step>
<Step title="Send">

**Send**, then confirm. A message says how many messages were handed to the delivery service, and which students have no contact.

</Step>
</Procedure>

Contacts come from the student's record (father's and mother's phones, e-mail address): complete them in [batch editing](/students/batch-edit) or the student record.
