<Prerequisite>

- An account in the **Chargés de discipline** (discipline officers) group; if it is limited to class groups, only their classes are offered (see [roles and access](/users/roles#limit-an-account-to-class-groups)).
- The [disciplinary grid](/discipline/rules) set up by the administration.

</Prerequisite>

<Procedure>
<Step title="Open the entry page">

On the home page, **Infos discipline**. Choose the **period**, then the **class**: the student list shows what has already been entered.

<DocScreenshot src="web/roles/discipline-saisie" alt="Discipline entry: late arrivals, absences in hours, detention, suggested sanction">
  <Hotspot number={1} x={26} y={40} label="Absences (hours)" />
  <Hotspot number={2} x={54} y={38} label="Suggested sanction" />
</DocScreenshot>

</Step>
<Step title="Enter the figures">

For each student: **late arrivals** and **absences in hours** (total and justified), **hours of detention**, warning or reprimand, days of exclusion. Empty boxes count as 0; justified figures above the total are flagged in red.

The **sanction suggested** by the grid appears straight away. It is never imposed: **Insert** copies it into the observation, which you can complete.

</Step>
<Step title="Special sanctions and observation">

Tick **disciplinary board**, **parents summoned** or **cleaning duty** if needed: the mention is added to the observation, and removed if you untick. **Ban from the honour roll** asks for confirmation.

<DocScreenshot src="web/roles/discipline-observation" alt="Observation completed with the inserted sanction and the parents' summons" />

</Step>
<Step title="Save">

Click **Submit**. The page returns to the same class and period, with a confirmation message. Only the students shown are saved.

<DocScreenshot src="web/roles/discipline-enregistre" alt="Message: discipline information for Term 1 - Tle D saved" />

</Step>
</Procedure>

<ExpectedResult>

The information appears on the period's report cards and in class councils. **Disciplinary grid**, top right, shows the rules at any time.

</ExpectedResult>
