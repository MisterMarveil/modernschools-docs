The **disciplinary grid** links a reason (detentions, unexcused absences, warnings…) and a quantity to a sanction.

<DocScreenshot src="web/discipline/grid" alt="Disciplinary grid: rules, reasons, minimum and maximum quantities, unit, sanction" />

Each rule states:

- the **reasons** taken into account;
- a **minimum and maximum quantity** and its **unit** (for example 12 to 16 hours of unexcused absence);
- the matching **sanction** (warning, reprimand, temporary exclusion, parents summoned…).

Rules are found under **Assessment grids → Disciplinary grid** and **Disciplinary rules**.

<DocScreenshot src="web/discipline/rules" alt="List of disciplinary rules and their sanction" />

:::info
Adapt the grid to the school rules before the first class council: it feeds the disciplinary remarks on report cards.
:::
