<Procedure>
<Step title="Add a subject">

**Settings → Structures → Subjects → Add**. Choose the **Parent group** (for example *Matières Scientifiques*), then enter the **name** and **short name**, in French and in the **English version** tab. Click **Create**.

<DocScreenshot src="web/subjects/create" alt="Subject creation form: parent group, name and short name in French and English" />

</Step>
<Step title="Adjust the coefficient">

The coefficient is set per class. The [initial setup](/structure/initial-setup) templates set it for each subject; a difference is corrected in the subject settings of the class concerned. Teachers see the coefficient at the top of their mark sheet and can report an anomaly.

</Step>
</Procedure>

:::tip
The short name appears on report cards and results minutes: keep it short (*Maths*, *Hist/Geo*).
:::
