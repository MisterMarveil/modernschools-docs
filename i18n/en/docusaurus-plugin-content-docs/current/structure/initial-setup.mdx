<Prerequisite>

An administrator account. Open **Settings**, then **Configuration initiale → Ouvrir** (initial setup).

</Prerequisite>

<DocScreenshot src="web/setup/initial-setup" alt="Initial setup screen: school templates, classes to tick, school year, school status" />

<Procedure>
<Step title="Choose the template">

Choose the tab for your type of school: **Enseignement général** (general), **Enseignement professionnel** (vocational) or **Enseignement technique** (technical). The template shows its periods (terms or semesters), assessments and number of subjects.

</Step>
<Step title="Tick the classes">

Tick your school's classes, cycle by cycle (**tout cocher**, or tick/untick a cycle). Classes already created are greyed out and marked *déjà créée*.

</Step>
<Step title="Set the year and create">

Check the **school year** (for example 2026-2027), then click **Créer les classes cochées** (create the ticked classes).

</Step>
</Procedure>

<ExpectedResult>

A summary shows what was created and what already existed. The **Votre établissement** box on the right counts classes, subjects, coefficients, periods, time slots, students and teachers.

</ExpectedResult>

:::info Nothing is overwritten
Existing data is never changed: you can come back to add classes. Coefficients and period dates are adjusted afterwards in the usual menus.
:::
