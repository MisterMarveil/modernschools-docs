## Class groups

Classes are arranged in a tree of **class groups**: the school, then cycles, then levels. For example: *Etablissement* → *1er Cycle* → *6èmes*. Statistics and some documents rely on this tree.

<DocScreenshot src="web/structure/class-groups" alt="Class groups list: Etablissement, 1er Cycle, 6èmes, 5èmes, 3èmes, 2nd Cycle, Tles" />

## Classes

A **class** (for example *6eme* or *Tle D*) belongs to a class group, has a default language and, where needed, an exam code. Students are enrolled in a class.

## Subjects and coefficients

**Subjects** are grouped into subject groups (literary, science, complementary). A subject's **coefficient** depends on the class: mathematics may count 4 in 5eme and 5 in Tle D.

## Assessment periods

The year (*Annuelle* period) is split into terms or semesters, each assessed through assessment types: see [understand periods](/evaluations/periods).
