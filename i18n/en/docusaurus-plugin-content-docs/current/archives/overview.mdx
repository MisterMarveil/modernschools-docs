Each school year of a school runs in **its own space**, with its own address. When a new year starts, the past year's space is kept: it is an **archive**.

## Good to know

- [Find my school](/getting-started/find-school) always opens the **current year's** space. It never opens an archive instead of a year being prepared.
- Report cards, marks and documents of an archived year stay as produced: a change to the school's identity does not alter them.
- To consult an archive, ask the school administrator for its address or make a request from [My school](/onboarding/existing-school).
