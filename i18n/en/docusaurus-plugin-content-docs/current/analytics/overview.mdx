**REPORTS → Statistiques** opens the statistics dashboard.

<DocScreenshot src="web/analytics/overview" alt="Statistics dashboard: total enrolment, gender split and enrolment per class" />

- The **filters** at the top narrow the analysis: class group, teacher, class, subject, gender, period; **Regrouper par** changes the grouping. Click **Filtrer** to apply.
- The left menu opens the **Stats Générales**, **Effectifs** (enrolment), **Performances**, **Enseignants** (teachers), **Matières** (subjects) and **Évolution** views.
- **Exports** downloads the data shown.

Figures cover the current year's students; tick **Inclure uniquement les élèves actifs** to leave out students who have left.
