**REPORTS → Statistiques** opens the statistics dashboard.

<DocScreenshot src="web/analytics/overview" alt="Statistics dashboard: headcount, pass rate, overall average and mark-entry rate" />

## Filtering

The **filters** at the top narrow the analysis: period, class group, class, teacher, subject, gender. Click **Apply filters**, or **Reset** to go back to the default settings.

- By default the view covers the **current period**; pick **All periods** for the whole year. The period used and its dates are shown under the title.
- Filters are **kept** when you move from one view to another through the left menu.
- **Active students only** leaves out students who have left; **Include unknown gender** counts students whose gender is not recorded.

The logo, top left, takes you back to the reports page.

## The views

- **Overview**: headcount, pass rate (average ≥ 10), overall average, mark-entry rate, then charts and rankings.
- **Headcount**: breakdown by gender, class and group.
- **Performance**: average by class, **distribution of averages** by band, **results by class and gender** (ranked, passed, pass rate for boys and girls, plus a whole-school row).
- **Teachers**: **mark entry** (below), then student results by teacher.
- **Subjects** and **Trends**: averages by subject, and how they change from one period to the next.

## Mark entry by teacher

<DocScreenshot src="web/analytics/insertion-notes" alt="Mark-entry rate by teacher and evaluation type" />

For each period that has started, an **expected sheet** is one class and one subject from the teacher's timetable, for one evaluation type (Harm. 1, Harm. 2…). It counts as **entered** as soon as one mark is recorded, and as **late** once the period's entry deadline (or else its end) has passed with no mark.

The view gives the overall mark-entry rate, a table by teacher with one column per evaluation type (least advanced first), totals by type and by period, and the list of **late sheets to follow up**.

:::tip
Expected sheets come from the timetable: a teacher who is not on the timetable does not appear here.
:::

## Exporting

**Export** offers:

- **Tables on this view (Excel, CSV)**: every table shown, with the filters applied, in a file that opens directly in Excel;
- **Print or save as PDF**: the view laid out for printing (A4 landscape), without menus or filters; choose "Save as PDF" in the print window.
