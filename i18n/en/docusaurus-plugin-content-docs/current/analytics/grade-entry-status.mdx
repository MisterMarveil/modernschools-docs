Before generating [report cards](/documents/report-cards), check that every grade sheet has been entered.

<Procedure>
<Step title="Open the grade entry status">

**REPORTS → Grade entry status** (Performance column).

</Step>
<Step title="Choose the period">

The current period is preselected. Change it if needed, or choose a teacher, then **Refresh**.

<DocScreenshot src="web/analytics/grade-entry-overview" alt="Grade entry status: filters, overall entry rate, overdue sheets, average completion, charts" />

</Step>
<Step title="Read the indicators">

- **Overall entry rate**: sheets entered over sheets expected (one sheet = one class, one subject, one evaluation).
- **Overdue sheets**: not entered after the period's deadline.
- **Average completion**: share of students graded in the entered sheets.
- **Teachers needing attention**, then the list of **overdue sheets**: class, subject, days overdue.

<DocScreenshot src="web/analytics/grade-entry-attention" alt="Teachers needing attention: expected, entered and overdue sheets, View and Notify buttons" />

</Step>
<Step title="Remind a teacher">

**Notify** opens an editable message, sent to the teacher through the school's messaging (WhatsApp or e-mail). The teacher's phone number or e-mail must be on their record (Users).

<DocScreenshot src="web/analytics/grade-entry-notify-result" alt="Send a notification window, with the message saying sending is not enabled yet" />

</Step>
</Procedure>

:::info Messaging
Sending messages is enabled school by school by ModernSchools. Until it is, the window says so and nothing is sent (since version 2026.11.17; before, the button showed a success without sending anything).
:::

## A teacher's detail

Choose the teacher in the filter: their sheets by status and each sheet's completion rate.

<DocScreenshot src="web/analytics/grade-entry-teacher" alt="A teacher's detail: expected, entered and overdue sheets, breakdown by status" />

## Export

**Export CSV** (top right) produces a file with the indicators, the per-teacher statistics and the overdue sheets, readable in a spreadsheet.
