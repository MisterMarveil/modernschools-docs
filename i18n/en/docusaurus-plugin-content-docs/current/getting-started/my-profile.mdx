<Procedure>
<Step title="Open your profile">

From the home screen, click **MY PROFILE**.

<DocScreenshot src="web/profile/edit" alt="Profile editing: personal information, language preference, profile picture" />

</Step>
<Step title="Update">

- **Personal information**: first names, surname, e-mail, date and place of birth, gender, phone. The username cannot be changed.
- **Language preference**: the application language.
- **Profile picture**: JPEG, PNG, GIF or WebP, 5 MB at most.
- **Password change**, further down: enter your current password, then the new one.

</Step>
<Step title="Save">

Save at the bottom of the page.

</Step>
</Procedure>
