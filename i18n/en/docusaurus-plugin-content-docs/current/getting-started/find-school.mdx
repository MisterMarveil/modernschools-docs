<Prerequisite>

No account is needed. Your school must have been published in the directory by the BRIDGE team.

</Prerequisite>

<Procedure>
<Step title="Open “Find my school”">

On `www.modernschools.online`, click **Sign in** (top right) or open **Find my school**.

</Step>
<Step title="Search for the school">

Type at least **3 characters**: the school's name, town or code. Search ignores capitals and accents; at most 20 results are shown.

<DocScreenshot src="portal/find-school/results" alt="Search “lycee demo”: Lycée Démonstration ModernSchools, Yaoundé, is found">
  <Hotspot number={1} x={47} y={33} label="Name, town or code" />
  <Hotspot number={2} x={70} y={43} label="Open the school" />
</DocScreenshot>

</Step>
<Step title="Open the school">

Click **Open**: you reach your school's address, where you [sign in](/getting-started/sign-in).

</Step>
</Procedure>

## With a school code

Your school may give you a link such as `www.modernschools.online/join/CODE`. It opens the school's card directly:

<DocScreenshot src="portal/find-school/join" alt="Card of Lycée Démonstration ModernSchools opened by its code, with the Open ModernSchools button" />

## School status

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Active | The school is in service: the button leads to its current-year address. |
| Preparing | Set-up is in progress: no address is given yet. |
| Suspended | Access is temporarily closed: contact your school. |
| Archived | Only past years can be viewed, read-only. |

<ExpectedResult>

You are on your school's sign-in page. Bookmark it.

</ExpectedResult>
