ModernSchools runs your school's day-to-day academic life: students, classes, teachers, marks, report cards, class councils and official documents.

## The ModernSchools spaces

| Space | Address | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| Official website | `www.modernschools.online` | Discover ModernSchools, register a school, **find your school** |
| Your school | your school's own address, for example `https://your-school.modernschools.store` | Daily work: administration, records, marks, documents |
| Documentation | `docs.modernschools.online` | These guides |

Each school has **its own address** and its own accounts: you do not sign in on the official website but on your school's address. If you do not know it, [find your school](/getting-started/find-school).

## Where to start

- **Your school is joining ModernSchools**: [register a new school](/onboarding/new-school).
- **You run a school**: [sign in](/getting-started/sign-in), [understand the home screen](/getting-started/dashboard), then the [initial setup](/structure/initial-setup).
- **You are a teacher**: [sign in](/getting-started/sign-in), then [enter marks](/grades/enter-grades).

## The administrator's path

1. [Register the school](/onboarding/new-school) and have it set up by the BRIDGE team.
2. [Sign in](/getting-started/sign-in) and change the default password.
3. [Create classes from a template](/structure/initial-setup), then [import students](/students/import) and [teachers](/users/create-teacher).
4. [Check the assessment periods](/evaluations/configure-periods).
5. Follow [mark entry](/grades/enter-grades), [generate report cards](/documents/report-cards) and [hold class councils](/class-council/run).
