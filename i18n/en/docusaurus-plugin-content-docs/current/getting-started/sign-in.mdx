<Prerequisite>

- Your school's address (otherwise: [find my school](/getting-started/find-school)).
- Your username and password, given by the school administration. For an imported teacher, the username looks like `SURNAME First name`.

</Prerequisite>

<Procedure>
<Step title="Enter your username and password">

On your school's sign-in page, enter your **username** and **password**, then confirm.

<DocScreenshot src="web/sign-in/login" alt="School sign-in page: username, password, remember me">
  <Hotspot number={1} x={48} y={38} label="Username" />
  <Hotspot number={2} x={48} y={43} label="Password" />
</DocScreenshot>

</Step>
<Step title="First sign-in: check your account">

With a default password, ModernSchools opens the account verification screen (**Vérification du compte requise**): the application stays locked until the password is changed.

Check your **surname**, **first name**, **gender** and **language**; the phone number is optional. Enter your **new password** twice, then click **Enregistrer les informations** (save).

<DocScreenshot src="web/sign-in/password-change" alt="Restricted access screen: account check and choice of a new password">
  <Hotspot number={1} x={55} y={50} label="Your details" />
  <Hotspot number={2} x={47} y={70} label="Application language" />
</DocScreenshot>

</Step>
<Step title="Sign in again">

You are taken back to the sign-in page: sign in with the **new** password.

</Step>
</Procedure>

<ExpectedResult>

You reach the ModernSchools home screen for your role: [understand the home screen](/getting-started/dashboard).

</ExpectedResult>

:::warning Privacy
Never share your password, with a colleague or with support: ModernSchools support will never ask for it.
:::
